Connect TikTok
How to create the TikTok app (5 steps)
  1. On developers.tiktok.com, log in with your TikTok and go to Manage apps → Connect an app.
  2. Fill in name, icon, description, category and the website, Privacy Policy and Terms URLs.
  3. Add the products Login Kit (platform: Desktop) and Content Posting API.
  4. Scopes: user.info.basic and video.upload. In Login Kit, add the Redirect URI shown above.
  5. Copy the Client key and Client secret here, Save keys, then Connect TikTok. Until TikTok reviews the app, only accounts added as testers (in the app's Sandbox) can connect.
