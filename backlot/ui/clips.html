How it works

  1. Transcribes on your Mac (faster-whisper), timing every word.
  2. Cuts on whole sentences — the best moments by pace, energy and few pauses, or the whole video in parts.
  3. Renders 9:16 clips with word-by-word captions burned in.
  4. You download the ones you like — no Claude usage at all.
  1. Downloads (or receives) and transcribes the video, timing every word.
  2. Ranks the moments by hook, standalone sense, value and energy.
  3. You review: watch each moment, trim start and end, pick which ones go in.
  4. Renders vertical clips that follow the face, with word-by-word captions.

Summary
