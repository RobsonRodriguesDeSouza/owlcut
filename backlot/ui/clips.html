How it works

  1. Downloads and transcribes the video, timing every word.
  2. Ranks the moments by hook, standalone sense, value and energy.
  3. You review: watch each moment, trim start and end, pick which ones go in.
  4. Renders vertical clips that follow the face, with word-by-word captions.

Summary

Use your own videos, or ones you have permission to use. Reposting other people's content can break copyright law and platform rules.
