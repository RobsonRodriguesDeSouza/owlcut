OwlCut

OwlCut is a desktop application that runs on the creator's own computer. It turns long videos into short vertical clips, adds the creator's own watermark and captions, and helps them publish those clips to their own social media accounts.

TikTok integration

When the creator connects their TikTok account with Login Kit, OwlCut can send a clip they choose to their TikTok inbox (Content Posting API, video.upload). The creator then opens the draft in the TikTok app, edits it and decides whether to post it. OwlCut never posts anything without the creator's action.

Source code: github.com/RobsonRodriguesDeSouza/owlcut
